Privacy policy

"rclone backup" is a private tool used by a single person to back up their own files to their own Google Drive.

  • It accesses Google Drive only on behalf of its owner, after the owner signs in.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data with anyone. Files go directly from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking are used, by the tool or by this page.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
